How it works

Notes

  • DDing — the deep bass note in the centre.
  • 1–9Tone fields — numbered from the lowest note upwards, zig-zagging left and right.
  • B1 B2Bottom notes (D Kurd 12) — on the underside of the shell.

Percussion

  • TTak — a sharp fingertip tap on the shoulder of the ding, where the ding meets the shell.
  • KKnock — a dry knock on free metal between the notes, with a knuckle or a fingertip.
  • SSlap — flat fingers on free metal between two notes, a snare-like pop. It works on both sides: SL on the left, SR on the right.
  • PPalm — a soft flat palm on the ding: a deep, warm “dum”.
  • FFist — the side of a loose fist on free metal near the rim: a low thump.

The letters mark a zone, not one exact spot: any free metal in that area is right. A song can have more zones of one kind: S2, T2… In Create song → Place zones you drag each zone to where it is played in that song and pick its hand. Zones never sit on a note. On the stage a zone shows up about a beat before its stroke and fades out after. Songs without their own zones use the zones from My Handpan. With the microphone on, any clear percussive hit counts for T, K, S, P or F.

Teachers don't all use the same letters (some even swap Tak and Slap). The idea is always the same: the letter tells you the kind of stroke and roughly where.

Ghost notes, flams and mutes

  • (4)Ghost note — the stroke in brackets: a whisper-quiet touch, more feel than sound. It falls smaller and fainter, and a missed ghost note never counts as a miss.
  • *4Flam — a star before the stroke: a tiny grace stroke just before the main one, with the same hand.
  • MMute — rest a hand on the shell and the ringing stops. It is not scored.

Which hand

Blue = left hand Amber = right hand

Every stroke in a song has a hand, L or R, shown by its colour. These are the usual colours in handpan notation. In the song builder the small L / R under each stroke is the hand.

The hand is a suggestion. Most handpan rhythms alternate hands (left, right, left…), which keeps fast parts easy. Each hand can play up to two notes at the same time.

Left-handed? Turn on Mirror the strokes in My Handpan.

Beats and bars

Music is counted in steady beats, like tapping your foot. A bar groups a few beats, usually four. Only the number of beats per bar matters here: 4/4 is 4 beats, 3/4 is 3, and 6/8 is counted as 6.

How long a stroke lasts

A stroke lasts until the next one starts. Splitting a beat evenly gives the usual lengths:

1 beat4quarter note
½ beat4:0.5 or 4_eighth note
⅓ beat4:1/3triplet
¼ beat4:0.25 or 4=sixteenth note
¾ beat4:0.75dotted eighth
2 beats4:2half note

A rest - is one beat of silence. Each beat can be split its own way (two quick strokes, then a triplet), as long as every beat adds up to one.

Modes

Listen plays the song so you can sit back and enjoy it. Practice scores every stroke from the first note (Perfect, Good, Miss). Wait for note lets the notes fly in and then stops on each one until you play it — play in time and it never stops. Tuner listens to your handpan and shows how many cents each note is from A4 = 440 Hz.

Speed goes down to 0.1×. Use [ and ] to change it while you play. After a run, Smart loop repeats your trickiest bar: slower first, faster each time you play it clean.

Song tape

Under the handpan the strokes scroll past as numbers, a little of what you just played and more of what comes next. Chords are stacked. In Practice and Wait for note each stroke turns teal for Perfect, violet for Good and red for Miss. Press Tour to watch the demo tour again.

Microphone (beta)

Turn the microphone on and play your real handpan: tone fields are recognised by pitch, and any clear percussive hit counts for T, K, S, P or F. Turn the song sound (speaker button) off or use headphones so the app does not hear itself.

Writing songs

In New song / Edit song your song is shown as boxes, one box per beat: a half-beat stroke takes half a box, strokes played together are stacked, and the small L / R under each stroke is the hand. Tap the handpan to add strokes, tap a box to add after it, and × to remove it.

Prefer typing? Edit as text shows the same song as text: 4L 8R - | 4L+2R 8R - — space separates strokes, + plays together, L/R picks the hand, :0.5 makes a stroke half a beat, - is a one-beat rest, | is a bar line, # starts a section title. (4) is a ghost note, *4 a flam and M a mute.

Percussion has its own zones in every song: switch to Place zones under the handpan, drag a zone to where it is played, tap it to pick its hand, or add another one (S2, T2…).

To give a song to a friend: Edit song → Send as a link. Opening the link adds the song to their My songs.

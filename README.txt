ETHEREAL HANDPAN · HAND DRUM TRAINER
====================================

WHAT IS THIS APP?

A sister app to Ethereal Drum (Tongue Drum Trainer), made for handpans and hand
drums. Watch strokes fall into lanes (or fly towards the handpan), listen to the
song, then play along with your own drum.

Instruments (A4 = 440 Hz):
- D Kurd 9   D3 | A3 Bb3 C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4
- D Kurd 10  D3 | A3 Bb3 C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 C5
- D Kurd 12  D Kurd 10 + two bottom notes (F3 G3 by default; change them in
             My Handpan, because makers differ)

Tone fields are numbered from the lowest note up, zig-zagging around the ding:
even numbers on the left, odd numbers on the right.

Layout 1 (default, the most common): 2 = Bb and 1 = A share the bottom, Bb for
the left hand and A for the right. Layout 2: note 1 alone at the bottom.
The two rotate buttons on the stage turn the handpan by half a step,
anticlockwise or clockwise (the same pair is next to the handpan in New song /
Edit song). My Handpan has both layouts as buttons.


STROKES

D   Ding (Doum)  the bass note in the centre
1-9 Tone fields
B1  B2           bottom notes (D Kurd 12)
T   Tak / Tek    fingertip tap next to the ding, usually right hand
K   Ka           the same tap with the other hand, usually left
S   Slap         a sharp pop on the outer shoulder. There is a slap zone under
                 each hand: SL (left, between 4 and 6) and SR, its mirror image
                 on the right, so both hands reach the same distance. Both
                 sound the same.

T, K and S are drawn on the handpan as dashed circles, but only when the song
uses them. In My Handpan you can drag them to where you really play them,
mirror T and K for left-handed playing, or turn percussion off.

Blue notes = left hand, amber notes = right hand (like the hand colours in the
mobile app you liked). You can switch to one colour per note in My Handpan.


MODES

Listen         sit back: the app plays the song and the handpan lights up.
               Tap along if you like, nothing is scored.
Practice       play along in time. Every stroke, from the first note, is
               scored Perfect / Good / Miss, big at the top of the stage and
               on the song tape. The speaker button plays the song quietly
               along with you (or turns it off).
               Tick "Wait for note" and the notes fly in and then stop on each
               one until you play it (chords wait for every note). Play in
               time and it never stops.
Tuner          turns on the microphone and shows which note you struck and
               how many cents it is from A4 = 440 Hz (median of several
               readings while the note rings).

Speed: pick 0.25x to 1.5x in the green Speed list, or nudge it with [ and ]
on the keyboard (0.1x to 1.5x).

Donate opens the Ko-fi page (same as in Ethereal Drum).

Display: Drum centre (notes fly along glowing rays to the handpan, with the
same stars, glow and particle trails as Ethereal Drum) or Falling lanes. On a
phone, Falling lanes puts the lanes above the handpan, and Focus Mode makes
the stage fill the screen. Phones use Ethereal Drum's lean performance
profile, so particle trails are switched off there for smooth playback.

Song tape: under the handpan (Drum centre) the strokes scroll past as numbers,
a little of what you just played on the left and what comes next on the right,
chords stacked. In Practice and Wait for note each stroke lights up teal
(Perfect), violet (Good) or red (Miss), with a "Perfect x12" pop like a game.
In Focus Mode the song name sits under the tape.
Swipe the tape left or right (finger or mouse) to move back or forward in
the song.

Demo tour: the first time you open the app, Interstellar plays while the
handpan slowly drifts round like a saucer in space and a short tour points out
the controls. Press Tour (next to ?) to watch it again. The sound starts with
your first tap or click (browsers do not allow sound before that).

On a phone: Play opens the full-screen view (Back or Stop leaves it), the
Speed button on the stage goes 1x - 0.75x - 0.5x - 0.25x, and tapping the song
name there lets you pause and pick another song. The screen stays awake while
you play.

A-B loop: Set A, move on, Set B, then Loop A-B to repeat one section.
Count-in: one bar of clicks before the song (not used in Wait for note).
The metronome is a short wooden tick (a little higher on beat 1).


HOW TO PLAY ALONG

- Tap or click the handpan (multi-touch works for chords on a phone/tablet)
- Keyboard: Space play/pause, D ding, 1-9 tone fields, 0 and - bottom notes,
  T K S strokes (S plays the slap the song asks for, Shift+S the right one),
  [ ] speed
- Microphone (beta): turn it on in Practice and play your real handpan. Listen
  never uses the microphone, and the tuner switches it off again when you leave
  it. Notes you tap on screen are not counted twice when the phone's speaker
  plays them. Tone fields are
  recognised by pitch; any clear percussive hit counts for T, K or S. Use
  headphones in Demo so the app does not hear itself. The browser asks for
  permission the first time.


WRITING SONGS

Open New song (+) or Edit song -> it saves a copy to My songs. The song is
shown as boxes, one box per beat: a half-beat stroke takes half a box, strokes
played together are stacked, and the small L / R under each stroke is the
hand. Tap the handpan to add strokes, tap a box to add after it, x removes it.
"Edit as text" shows the same song as text, for typing:

  4L 8R - | 4L+2R 8R -

  space  separates strokes
  +      play together (a chord)
  L / R  after a stroke: which hand (optional - the app picks a side)
         SL / SR = slap with the left / right hand (left / right slap zone)
  :0.5   half a beat   (:0.25 quarter, :2 two beats, :1/3 a triplet)
  -      one beat rest  (0 and . work too)
  |      bar line
  #      comment until the end of the line

My songs are stored in this browser only. Use Export selected song to keep a
.handsong backup, and Import song to load one.

The All / Easy / Medium / Hard chips under Search songs filter the list.

"Low notes only" songs use just the ding and fields 1-6 (D3 A3 Bb3 C4 D4 E4
F4), so they work on every D Kurd.

Add your own demo songs by putting .handsong files into demo-songs. The START
launchers rebuild app-files/song-catalog.js automatically. You can also run:
  python scripts/build-song-catalog.py


HOW TO START

The simplest way: double-click index.html.

Windows: START_TRAINER_Win.bat (rebuilds the demo-song catalogue, opens the app
         and keeps watching demo-songs while the window is open)
macOS:   START_macOS.command (needs Python 3)
Linux:   sh START_Linux.sh   (needs Python 3)

On GitHub Pages it works the same way as Ethereal Drum: commit .handsong files
into demo-songs and the workflow rebuilds the catalogue. Copy
scripts/song-catalog.github-workflow.yml to .github/workflows/song-catalog.yml
once when you create the repository.


COMMUNITY SONGS
---------------
Side menu > Community songs: anyone can play them, no account needed.
"Share my song" (in that window) sends one of your own songs (My songs) for review.

Where sent songs go: the same private Google Drive review folder as Ethereal
Drum, through the same Google Apps Script. Handpan songs arrive as
handpan-<title>-<date>.handsong.

One-time setup, so the script also accepts handpan songs:
  1. Open script.google.com and the Ethereal Drum upload project.
  2. Replace all its code with app-files/owner-tools/GOOGLE_DRIVE_UPLOAD_SCRIPT.gs
     (the same file is in the Tongue Drum folder), then Save.
  3. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil (Edit) > Version: New version > Deploy.
     The web-app link stays the same, so nothing else changes.
No Cloudflare is involved.

Publishing an approved song: download it from Drive and put it in
community-songs/. On GitHub: Add file > Upload files, inside community-songs.
The "Update song catalog" action (.github/workflows/song-catalog.yml) rebuilds
app-files/song-catalog.js and GitHub Pages updates a minute or two later.
(Settings > Actions > General > Workflow permissions must be "Read and write",
as for Ethereal Drum.) On your computer, START_TRAINER does the same live.

TABLETS AND PHONES
------------------
On a tablet the song list slides in from the left (☰) and everything fits on
one screen. The ⛶ button hides the system bars (clock, battery). On a phone the
Play button on the stage opens the full-screen practice view.
The little ? buttons explain the A-B loop and the microphone.

COMMUNITY SONGS
===============

Approved .handsong files go in this folder. They appear in the app under
"Community songs" in the side menu.

On GitHub: upload the file here and the "Update song catalog" action rebuilds
app-files/song-catalog.js by itself (see README.txt, section Community songs).

On your computer: keep START_TRAINER open and the gallery updates live.

Visitors cannot add files here. They send a song from the app (Community songs >
Share my song); it lands in your private Google Drive review folder, and you move
the ones you approve into this folder.
